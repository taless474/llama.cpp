--- trend interpretation rules (a-priori) ---
- oversubscription:
    delta_pct(n=1) < delta_pct(n=2) < delta_pct(n=4) (strict monotonic)
    AND max - min >= 1.0%
    Reading: prior C_2x4 HPX overhead was amplified by kernel-thread
    oversubscription. Orchestration overhead alone is small.
- orchestration_or_waiter_path:
    max - min < 1.0%
    Reading: HPX overhead is intrinsic to serving-level orchestration
    or waiter path, not to oversubscription. C_2x4 in the matrix was
    not confounded by thread pressure.
- mixed:
    delta_pct trend is non-monotonic across n_threads = {1, 2, 4}.
    Reading: both effects contribute; cannot cleanly attribute.
- incomplete:
    any thread setting missing or its correctness state is FAIL.
    Reading: do not interpret timing.

--- caveats ---
- The sweep OVERALL gate is correctness for all three thread settings.
  Timing numbers above are descriptive and do not gate anything.
- Δ % is (hpx_median - std_median) / std_median * 100. Sub-1% deltas
  inside the CV band are not interpretable as 'different'.
- Timing rows for thread settings whose correctness is FAIL or MISSING
  are shown as '<suppressed>'.
- This is still C_2x4: waiter pressure is held constant. The sweep
  reduces kernel-thread oversubscription, not queueing pressure.
- n_threads=1 may use a different ggml/single-thread path. If the
  shrink is concentrated at n=1 only, prefer the 'mixed' or
  'incomplete' reading over a clean 'oversubscription' label.
- If the trend label is 'orchestration_or_waiter_path', the likely
  source is serving / HPX orchestration or waiter-path overhead.
- If the trend label is 'oversubscription', the prior C_2x4 overhead
  was likely amplified by oversubscription.
- This protocol measures THIS serving-bench harness on THIS machine,
  with THIS build, THIS model, THIS prompt, THIS generation length.
  It is not a measurement of llama.cpp performance, not a measurement
  of HPX scheduler quality in general, and not a recommendation for
  or against either backend.
