--- caveats ---
- The matrix OVERALL gate is correctness for all four cells. Timing
  numbers above are descriptive and do not gate anything.
- Cross-cell deltas (e.g. C is slower than B) reflect oversubscription
  shape, not anything HPX-vs-std. Only within-cell std-vs-hpx deltas
  speak to the protocol's question.
- Δ % is (hpx_median - std_median) / std_median * 100. Sub-1% deltas
  inside the CV band are not interpretable as 'different'.
- Timing rows for cells whose correctness is FAIL or MISSING are
  shown as '<suppressed>' to keep readers from interpreting timing
  over an uncertain correctness state.
- This protocol measures the HPX-vs-std overhead of THIS serving-bench
  harness on THIS machine, with THIS build, THIS model, THIS prompt,
  THIS generation length. It is not a measurement of llama.cpp
  performance, not a measurement of HPX scheduler quality in general,
  and not a recommendation for or against either backend.
