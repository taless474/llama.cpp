--- caveats ---
This is one bench-side leg of the (2, 4, 12) HPX waiter-pressure slice.
Cross-backend gates live in comparison_hpx_waiters/comparison_summary.txt.
All 12 requests are correctness-gated. No warmup separation.
Timing stats above are descriptive only; gates do not depend on them.
Oversubscription (2 contexts x 4 threads = 8 kernel threads on a 4-core box,
with 4 in-flight at saturation) is more severe than the (2, 2) slice and is
expected. It will slow each decode but must not change tokens.
Trace lines are event-ordered but not timestamped; we do NOT claim true
overlap of in-flight requests in time.
This slice does NOT establish FIFO waiter ordering. backend_hpx.cpp uses
std::deque<hpx::promise> with front-pop (the structural intent is FIFO),
but no queued/wake trace events are emitted today, so FIFO is not
observable from the trace and is not gated here.
This slice does NOT establish per-request wait time before acquire.
